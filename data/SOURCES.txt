Boundary data sources

- World countries: johan/world.geo.json, derived from Natural Earth public-domain data.
  https://github.com/johan/world.geo.json
- China provincial boundaries: Alibaba Cloud DataV geographic boundary service.
  https://geo.datav.aliyun.com/areas_v3/bound/100000_full.json
- ISO alpha-2/alpha-3 mapping: country-data package (MIT License).
  https://www.npmjs.com/package/country-data
- City search and map labels: GeoNames cities5000 dataset (CC BY 4.0).
  https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
- Leaflet 1.9.4 is bundled locally (BSD 2-Clause License).
  https://leafletjs.com/
  See ../vendor/leaflet-LICENSE.txt
